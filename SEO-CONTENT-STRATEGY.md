# IJ Langa Consulting SEO & Content Strategy

## Primary SEO goal
Build qualified organic visibility for IJ Langa Consulting for accounting, bookkeeping, tax/SARS, CIPC/company secretarial, payroll, compliance, CSD/COIDA, B-BBEE and business consulting services in South Africa, with strong local relevance for Ermelo/Mpumalanga.

## Primary audiences
- South African small and medium businesses needing accounting and bookkeeping support.
- Business owners needing SARS, VAT, PAYE, UIF, CIPC and compliance assistance.
- New entrepreneurs needing company registration, CSD, COIDA, B-BBEE and business-plan support.
- Existing clients needing payroll, financial statements, tax returns and recurring compliance services.

## Content pillars
1. Accounting & bookkeeping
2. Tax, SARS & VAT compliance
3. Company registration & CIPC services
4. Payroll, PAYE, UIF & SDL
5. Business compliance: CSD, COIDA, B-BBEE and related registrations
6. Tender readiness and business consulting
7. Practical South African business guides and compliance explainers

## Recommended landing-page structure
Create dedicated, genuinely useful pages as the catalogue grows. Use clean slugs and one canonical URL per page:

- /services/accounting-bookkeeping/
- /services/tax-sars/
- /services/company-registration/
- /services/cipc-secretarial/
- /services/payroll/
- /services/csd-registration/
- /services/coida-registration/
- /services/bbbee/
- /services/business-plans/
- /services/tender-support/

Each page should have one H1, a unique title and meta description, a clear service explanation, who the service is for, requirements, process, pricing/quote CTA where appropriate, FAQs based on real customer questions, and links to closely related services.

## Internal-linking model
Home → Service category → Detailed service page → Related service → Quote/contact CTA.

Every future service page should link back to the main services catalogue and to at least two closely related service pages. Avoid artificial keyword-rich anchor text; use descriptive, natural anchors such as "VAT registration", "monthly bookkeeping", "CSD registration" and "payroll services" when those are actually the destination topics.

## Publishing cadence
- 1 authoritative service/guide page per week during the first 12 weeks.
- 2 shorter FAQ/supporting articles per month after the core pages are established.
- Refresh regulatory content whenever SARS, CIPC, COIDA, B-BBEE or other official requirements change.

## Local SEO
Keep the business name, website, contact details and service area consistent across the website and legitimate business profiles. Build useful local references around Ermelo, Mpumalanga and South Africa without creating thin doorway pages for nearby towns.

## Backlink plan
Build backlinks through genuine business relationships and useful resources rather than bulk link packages or automated submissions.

Priority sources:
- Business and professional directories where IJ Langa Consulting is legitimately eligible to appear.
- Local business/community organisations and relevant chambers or associations.
- Supplier and professional profiles that the business actually owns or participates in.
- Partnerships with legitimate local business networks, entrepreneurship programmes and training initiatives.
- Original guides and checklists that other South African small-business sites can reference naturally.
- Case studies and client success stories where the client has given permission and the claims can be substantiated.

For each outreach opportunity, record the referring domain, target URL, anchor/context, date contacted, status and whether the link is live. Do not pay for or create large numbers of low-quality, irrelevant links.

## Content quality rules
- Write for customers first, not search engines.
- Do not publish near-duplicate service pages for different towns.
- Do not invent regulatory requirements, prices or credentials.
- Cite official SARS/CIPC/government sources when explaining legal or regulatory requirements.
- Add original examples, checklists and practical explanations.
- Update pages when regulations or service requirements change.

## Measurement
Use Google Search Console to monitor indexing, queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, page performance and Core Web Vitals. Review the sitemap and URL Inspection reports after major releases. Search Console verification is a separate ownership step and requires the site's Google verification method/token or DNS TXT record supplied by Google.
